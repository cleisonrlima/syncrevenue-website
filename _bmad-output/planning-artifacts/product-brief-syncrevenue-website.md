# Product Brief: Sync Sirius Institutional Website

## Executive Summary

Travel agencies across the Americas are bleeding commission revenue they've already earned. Debit memos issued by airlines and GDS operators — often for commissions applied with slightly wrong parameters — drain $5,000 to $50,000 per month from agencies that lack tools to dispute them systematically. An estimated 15–25% of total commission revenue leaks silently through unmatched credits, missed tier upgrades, and manual reconciliation errors. The back-office status quo — spreadsheets, GDS-native single-system reports, generic accounting software — was never built for this problem.

SyncRevenue, the flagship product of Sync Sirius, Inc., changes this. A purpose-built commission management platform for travel agencies of all sizes, SyncRevenue ingests airline contracts across all major GDS (Amadeus, Sabre, Galileo, Worldspan), applies a configurable rule engine informed by an expert operations team and AI, and applies commissions with **99.99% accuracy** — virtually eliminating the debit memo cycle at its source. Agencies stop losing money they're owed. Revenue recovered, costs cut, cash flow stabilized.

The Sync Sirius institutional website is the primary commercial surface for SyncRevenue — generating qualified demo leads across English-, Spanish-, and Portuguese-speaking markets in the Americas. The site positions Sync Sirius as the technical and operational reference for commission management in the travel industry: trusted, modern, human.

---

## The Problem

Every travel agency that books air, hotel, or ground through a GDS faces the same back-office nightmare:

**Commission leakage is invisible until it's too late.** Airlines issue hundreds of tickets per week. Each booking triggers a commission calculation based on contract terms — fare class, route, carrier, period, tier, override — that vary by airline and change regularly. A single misconfigured parameter produces a wrong commission. BSP and ARC collect settlement within 10 days. The agency only discovers the error when a debit memo arrives — sometimes weeks later, sometimes after the credit period has closed.

**Debit memos are opaque and difficult to dispute.** Airlines use 500+ reason codes. Manual reconciliation against weekly BSP statements is time-consuming and error-prone. Most agencies absorb the loss rather than fight it.

**GDS-native tools don't solve this.** An agency using Amadeus gets Amadeus reporting. An agency using Sabre gets Sabre reporting. An agency using both gets two siloed systems with no unified commission view and no cross-GDS reconciliation.

**The cash flow math is punishing.** Agencies extend 30-day credit to corporate clients while BSP demands settlement in 10 days. Every recovered commission dollar is a dollar of working capital the agency does not need to fund from its own pocket.

The result: mid-market and boutique agencies in the Americas — the segment with the most to recover relative to their size and the least enterprise back-office tooling — are losing real money every month, treating it as unavoidable overhead.

---

## The Solution

SyncRevenue is a commission management platform that makes accuracy the default, not the goal.

The platform is built around a configurable airline contract engine: agencies load their specific contracts with all parameters — fare conditions, commission tiers, override rules, validity periods — across every GDS they operate. A rule engine processes every booking against the loaded contracts, applies the correct commission, and flags discrepancies before settlement. An expert operations team and AI layer validate edge cases and continuously refine accuracy.

**The result: 99.99% commission assertivity.** When commissions are applied correctly from the start, debit memos largely disappear. The recovery isn't downstream dispute management — it's upstream accuracy.

SyncRevenue surfaces through a modern, intuitive interface (deliberate contrast to legacy GDS back-office UX). Agencies get full visibility: unified commission view across all GDS, real-time tracking, custom reporting, and clear audit trails.

The site's role is to make agencies feel this outcome before they see the product. Every section — from the hero to the comparison table — is designed to surface the pain, credibly position the solution, and convert interest into a scheduled demo.

## Who This Serves

**Primary buyer:** Agency owner, CEO, or CFO of a travel agency or TMC operating in the Americas. They feel the commission leakage pain in the P&L. They want a quantified ROI conversation, not a feature tour. They need to trust the vendor before they share GDS credentials and contract data — security posture and data handling must be visible on the site before a demo is requested.

**Primary user:** Back-office manager or ticketing supervisor responsible for BSP/ARC reconciliation. They're currently spending hours per week on manual reconciliation and dreading Monday morning debit memo reports. SyncRevenue replaces their spreadsheet with a verified commission statement.

**Market:** Mid-market through enterprise — agencies with enough booking volume that commission leakage is a material P&L line, not a rounding error. LatAm agencies in this tier are strategically underserved by current tooling; US mid-market agencies lack multi-GDS unified options despite the mature market. Boutique/micro agencies (1–5 agents) are out of scope for initial positioning.

## Scope

**In scope (Phase 1 MVP):**
- Full multilingual landing page (EN, PT-BR, ES) with i18n from day one
- All sections: Hero, SyncRevenue, Comparison, Services, Team (placeholder), Demo Scheduler, Contact/Footer
- Functional demo request form with SMTP notification
- Navbar: transparent → solid scroll behavior, language switcher, Demo CTA
- Security baseline: rate limiting, Zod validation, no secrets in client bundle
- Trust signals: data security statement (security certification roadmap + contract insurance commitment), team credibility (even with placeholder photos), recognized client references/testimonials
- Free commission audit tool: prospect submits 30 days of BSP data → Sync Sirius delivers a leak analysis showing recoverable revenue → high-intent lead magnet before demo commitment

**In scope (Phases 2–3):**
- Real team photos and bios
- Animations and micro-interactions
- SEO: meta, OG, hreflang, sitemap
- Admin dashboard: lead management by status + locale, team CRUD

**Explicitly out of scope for website:**
- Customer portal or authenticated agency access
- Pricing page (demos qualify leads; pricing is a sales conversation)
- Blog or content marketing infrastructure
