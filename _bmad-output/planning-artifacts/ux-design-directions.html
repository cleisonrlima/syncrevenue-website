Design Direction Summary

Review each direction above, then choose one — or name elements to combine.

Direction 1
Dark-First Immersive
Full navy background, gradient CTAs, trust bar in hero. Strongest visual brand presence.
Best for: premium positioning, paid ad traffic that expects bold landing pages
Direction 2
Light Corporate Authority
White-dominant with demo form visible in hero. Most "enterprise SaaS" of the set.
Best for: corporate buyers who find dark sites visually aggressive
Direction 3
Bold Gradient Immersive
Glow effects, high energy, pain-focused headline. Most visually dramatic.
Caution: leans toward startup energy — review against premium positioning goal
Direction 4
Editorial Minimalist
Heavy typography, structural borders, restrained color. Most newspaper-inspired.
Best for: sophisticated buyers who value clarity over visual drama
Direction 5
Split-Screen SaaS
Dark left / light right. Full demo form visible on load. Classic proven B2B pattern.
Best for: high-intent paid ad traffic — removes scroll before demo CTA
Direction 6
Dark Minimal / Linear-Inspired
Ultra-restrained, grid texture, thin weight + bold contrast. Calm authority.
Best for: tech-forward buyers who associate restraint with quality
